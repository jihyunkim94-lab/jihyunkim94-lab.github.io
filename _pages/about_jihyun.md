Jihyun Kim is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Intelligent Semiconductor Engineering at Ajou University, where Kim Lab opens in September 2026.

Before joining Ajou, he was a postdoctoral fellow at the Research Laboratory of Electronics, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, and previously at the Department of Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering, Yonsei University. He received his B.S. in Chemical Engineering and his M.S. and Ph.D. in Advanced Materials Science and Engineering from Sungkyunkwan University.
